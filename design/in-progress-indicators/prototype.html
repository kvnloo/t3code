Mobile in-progress indicators: five prototypes

Problem: on the home list, the Working shelf sits below every active (Done, Approval, Input) thread and starts collapsed. With 13 Done threads waiting to be settled, running agents are a long scroll away, so the first screen shows only finished work. Desktop/web keeps its Working shelf in the sidebar, where it stays in reach.

Constraints carried into every variant: zero idle cost (hidden when nothing runs), no continuously repainting animation (upstream measured the mobile shimmer at about 70 mW), one tap to the working thread, and the same count as the Android Live Update ("3 working"). Tap the indicators to open them.
